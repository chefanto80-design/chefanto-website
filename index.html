My Story

The flavor of resilience

"My grandmother never wasted a thing. She opened the fridge, looked at what was there, and made magic. That's the recipe I carry everywhere."

I grew up in Romania, where a meal was an act of love and nothing went to waste. In Miami, I rebuilt my life — and turned that old-world wisdom into a new-world mission: helping one billion meals get cooked from what's already in your fridge.

Today I run pop-up dinners, build AI kitchen tools, and share every step of the journey — the wins, the burns, and the recipes.
